## 📖 How to Use (English)
1. **Download & Install:** Click the download button above, run the setup, and open the app.
2. **Upload Files:** Drag and drop your Books (Purchase Register) and Portal 2B Excel files into the application.
3. **Run Engine:** Click **Execute Matching Engine**. The software will automatically match all your invoices.
4. **Preview & Export:** Check the color-coded results in the Preview tab, and click **Export Results to Excel** to save your final report.
